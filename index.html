What is Pulma

Pulma is a handheld breath-analysis device paired with a mobile app. Breathe into the device for 10 seconds and its sensors read the pattern of volatile organic compounds (VOCs) in your breath. The reading is sent to the app over Bluetooth, which shows a lung cancer risk level and whether a visit to a doctor is recommended.
