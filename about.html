About Us

The team behind Pulma.

We are building Pulma to make a first check for lung cancer as simple as breathing.

Our Mission

Lung cancer is most treatable when it is caught early, yet most people are only checked once symptoms appear. Our mission is to close that gap with a breath test anyone can take in seconds — non-invasive, portable and easy to understand — so that people at risk are guided to a doctor sooner.

Pulma brings together hardware, software and clinical feedback: a handheld breath-analysis device, a mobile app that turns each reading into a clear result, and testing alongside physicians to shape how Pulma fits into real care.
