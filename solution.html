The Solution

A lung check as simple as breathing.

A handheld breath-analysis device and a mobile app, working as one.

Inside Pulma

Six parts, from the mouthpiece at the front to the vents at the back.

  1. MouthpieceBreathe in through the front of the head
  2. Touch displayShows the status and the score on the device
  3. BME688 gas sensorReads VOCs, humidity, temperature and pressure
  4. Controller boardRuns the scan and talks to the app over Bluetooth
  5. USB-C chargingCharges the device over USB-C
  6. Rear ventsBreath leaves through the back of the head

Follow One Breath

From the mouthpiece to the app, one scan in five steps.
