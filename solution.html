The Solution

A lung check as simple as breathing.

Pulma pairs a handheld breath-analysis device with a mobile app, turning a 10‑second breath sample into a clear lung cancer risk result.

The Device

The Pulma device is a compact, handheld unit designed for a quick breath test anywhere — in a clinic, in the community or at home.

  • Breath inletBreathe straight into the top of the device for 10 seconds.
  • Breath sensorsRead the pattern of volatile organic compounds (VOCs) in the sample, calibrated for humidity and temperature.
  • On-device displayA built-in screen on the front of the device.
  • BluetoothSends every reading to the Pulma app for analysis and storage.

How It Works

From breath to result in four steps.

1

Connect

Pair the Pulma device with the app over Bluetooth.

2

Breathe

Breathe into the device for 10 seconds, in a well-ventilated area.

3

Analyze

Sensors read the VOC pattern of the breath sample, calibrated for humidity and temperature.

4

Review

The app shows the risk level, the main finding and a recommended next step.

The Pulma App

The app guides each scan and turns the sensor reading into a result anyone can understand. Every scan is saved, so users can follow their history and trends over time.

Pulma is a pre-screening and wellness-tracking tool. A result is not a diagnosis; anyone flagged at risk is advised to see a doctor.
